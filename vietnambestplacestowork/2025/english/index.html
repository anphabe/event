CODE THE FUTURE

AI and automation are rewriting the rules of work, organizations in Vietnam face a defining set of strategic questions: Where do we stand on the “Lean & Learn” map? What are the “system bugs” preventing true organizational readiness? And most importantly, is it time to move beyond the old paradigms and proactively code the future we aspire to build?

The CODE THE FUTURE report unveils a comprehensive view of how Organizations are reprogramming for the new world of work in Vietnam, grounded in insights from tens of thousands of employees and hundreds of senior leaders nationwide. More than a data report, it is a call to action for visionary leaders ready to code the future with agility, foresight, and a steadfast belief in the power of people."

Alpha Talk

CRACKING THE INNOVATION CODE: HOW GENERATIONS POWER THE FUTURE OF WORK

Innovation is often seen as the product of technology - but in reality, it’s powered by people. With Boomers, Gen X, Millennials, and Gen Z working side by side, the greatest untapped source of innovation lies in generational diversity. Each generation brings unique strengths, from experience and pragmatism to agility and fresh perspectives.

This session reveals how organizations can crack the innovation code by harnessing these differences, breaking stereotypes, and building cross-generational teams that fuel creativity, resilience, and future growth.

Ms. THANH NGUYEN

Chief Happiness Officer
Anphabe

Ms. Thanh Nguyen is Chief Happiness Officer at Anphabe - the pioneer in Employer Branding and Happiness at Work solutions in Vietnam. With the mission “Building a happy Vietnamese workforce,” in August 2017 she became the first Vietnamese to receive the international “Contribution to the HR Community” award by HRD Asia Congress.

Before founding Anphabe, she was Regional Senior Brand Director for Asia, Africa and the Middle East at Unilever, and later Founder & CEO of an HR services company under Navigos Group.

Ms. LUU BAO VAN

Research Director
Intage Vietnam

Van holds a Master’s in Marketing, Sales and Services (Sorbonne University in partnership with IAE Paris & ESCP-EAP). She is a key leader at Intage - Japan’s No.1 market research firm and a top 10 global player.

With 10 years of qualitative and quantitative experience, Van is a familiar face to multinational and Vietnamese companies in market measurement, consumer trend analysis, and strategy planning.

Beyond research, she frequently speaks on consumer trends and tech-enabled research solutions, and contributes to training and teaching in the research field.

Ms. Rachele Focardi

Global Expert & Keynote Speaker; LinkedIn Top Voice; Top 6 Most Influential Women in the New World of Work; Member of MENSA International (The largest and oldest high-IQ society in the world)

Rachele Focardi is a global thought leader, bestselling author, and TEDx speaker dedicated to helping organizations build meaningful collaboration across generations. She is the Founder & CEO of XYZ@Work and Chair of the Multigenerational Workforce Committee at the ASEAN Human Development Organization (AHDO). She is also a renowned speaker on global talent and generational trends, regularly invited to deliver keynotes at top conferences and leadership summits around the world.

For more than two decades, Rachele has advised global corporations - including the majority of Fortune 100 companies, leading organizations across APAC and government bodies - helping them address future talent needs and maximize their strengths as employers to lay the foundation for successful Employer Branding and Talent Strategies.

Recognized among the Top 6 Most Influential Women in the New World of Work and recipient of the “Pioneer” Award for advancing generational equity, Rachele is also a Global LinkedIn Top Voice and proud Member of MENSA International - the world’s largest and oldest high-IQ society.

Mr. GEOVANNE BERTONHA

Chief Executive Officer
MTI Technology

Geovanne Bertonha is a pioneering technology leader and currently serves as the CEO of MTI Technology. With many years of experience in designing and leading digital transformation across Asia, he combines the power of Lean and Artificial Intelligence (AI) to optimize operations and drive innovation within organizations.

Under his leadership, MTI Technology has built a strong team of technology engineers, applying Agile and advanced technologies to deliver comprehensive solutions — from software development to specialized Digital Transformation projects.

Geovanne believes that people are at the heart of every technological advancement. He promotes a culture of continuous learning, encourages creativity and collaboration, and fosters a flexible work environment where ideas are nurtured and brought to life.

With an inspiring leadership style, he focuses not only on business performance but also on creating long-term impact, helping enterprises achieve sustainable growth and strengthen their position in the digital era.

Ms. TRINH MAI PHUONG

Personal Care HR Lead, Unilever Greater Asia Vietnam Head of HR, Unilever Vietnam

After pursuing a Master’s Degree in Educational Management in Australia, Ms. Trinh Mai Phuong came back to Vietnam and accelerated her career in Human Resources Management. She has 18 years of deep experience in both strategic, expertise and generalist roles for large FMCG companies, in diverse areas of expertise including: learning, talent and leadership development, HR business partnering, strategic HR planning and management.

Having a strong passion for lifelong learning, she strives for continuously innovating HR practices to enable the workforce to adopt agile learning, get prepared for the future and create more values to self and others. She has lived in Ukraine, Russia, Australia, Indonesia and can speak a number of languages. She is a strong advocate for diversity, inclusiveness and agility in the workplace.

Mr. NGUYEN VIET HA

General Manager
PepsiCo Foods Vietnam

Nguyen Viet Ha has more than 20 years of working experience holding senior commercial leadership roles in FMCG industry and now holding as General Manager of PepsiCo Foods Vietnam.

He demonstrated remarkable leadership by guiding his team through a series of ongoing transformations, ultimately achieving success even during the challenges posed by the Covid-19 pandemic. Under his stewardship, Lays has ascended to become the premier snack brand in Vietnam. Beyond his impressive business achievements, Ha has been a staunch supporter of PepsiCo Foods Vietnam's Employer Branding Strategy since 2020. He has played a pivotal role in cultivating the DARE TO WIN & CARE TO WIN culture within the core of PepsiCo, truly embodying the spirit of a captain leading his team to victory.

Ms. CAP THI MINH TRANG

HR Director, Schneider Electric Vietnam & Cambodia Schneider Electric

Ms. Cap Thi Minh Trang currently holds the position of HR Director, Schneider Electric Vietnam & Cambodia. With 20 years of working experiences, she has always played critical Human Resources Management roles in various types of organizations including local Human Resources Services companies and Multinational corporations such as Deloitte, America Indochina Management, British American Tobacco, Abbott Nutrition International & Schneider Electric. At Schneider Electric, Trang is a member of the Vietnam Cluster Leadership Team as well as Regional HR Leadership Team in East Asia and serves as the primary strategic partner to the Business leaders by developing Human Resources strategies, organization needs assessments and resulting Human Resources solutions.

Besides her HR professional work, Trang is also an owner of Capdesign, her own fashion brand targeting professional working women. This expertise has made her become a professional lecturer on Dress Code at John Robert Powers Vietnam since 2016.

Interestingly, Trang was born in a family of musicians and became a professional pianist since her early age. This actually makes her profile become very unique and the values that she brings to her stakeholders, colleagues and clients are also very exciting and diverse.
